Risk Management

Failure Modes & Vice Principal Controls

Every edge case has a defined institutional control and escalation path.

⚠️ Student Phone Battery Died

Control: Faculty or lab staff can tap "Manual Verify" on their own phone, select the student's roll number, and verify presence in 2 seconds.

📶 Total Campus Wi-Fi Outage

Control: System switches automatically to local SQLite storage. All scans continue offline and sync silently when connection returns.

📸 Attempted Proxy via WhatsApp Photo

Control: QR code refreshes every 4 seconds with cryptographic hashes. Photo sent by message expires before recipient can scan it.

⚖️ Disputed Defaulter Status

Control: System produces an immutable event log with exact timestamps, lecture topic, and teacher ID, ending arbitrary disputes.
